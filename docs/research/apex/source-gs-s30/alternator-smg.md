## Background
The Alternator is famous for its accuracy: featuring twin barrels that alternate fire from a single magazine, it suffers less of the barrel-wear that reduces accuracy in other firearms. However, its patented receiver and barrel assembly ensured that it could only be manufactured by Burrell Industries in conjunction with Emslie Tactical, hampering widespread adoption during the Frontier War. After Burrell’s collapse following the Blackout, the design was reverse-engineered by surviving competitors, and current models have reached nearly the same level of sophistication.
