## Background
Ah, the RE-45 Burst, a cherished member of my private collection. An energetic twist on an old favourite, recommended for connoisseurs who understand good things come to those who wait. Its charged bursts remind me of a particularly stiff drink I had on Salvo, long ago. Its flavor took a moment to hit but when it did, it was…devastating. A perfect pairing to the Apex Games, wouldn’t you say?
