## Background
For a most efficient experience, the Hemlok Breach AR is one I often choose from my collection. You might imagine the classic as an easy conversation with a well-mannered fellow, one you’ve known for a lifetime. The Breach, however, bears more resemblance to the coarse argument that ensues when a discussion gets a bit tempestuous. Fortunately, harsh words and heated quarrels are well-suited to the Games.
