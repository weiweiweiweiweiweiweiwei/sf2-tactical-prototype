Background

The latest in a proud line of standard-issue assault rifles, the R-301 Carbine is as reliable, versatile, and easy - to - control as its forebears, the R-101 and R201-SOAR. The series is almost as old as Frontier civilization – some say the first Carbines were built on Earth – and has been a mainstay in every Frontier conflict.
