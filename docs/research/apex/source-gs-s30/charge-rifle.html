Background

This portable beam cannon was originally designed to take down Titans in the field, so imagine what it can do to a human. The Charge Rifle’s signature orange beam starts charging on trigger - pull and fires when fully charged. This unique firing cadence makes accuracy essential - a poorly aimed shot will leave the operator vulnerable to return fire. A shot that lands, however, can finish the fight before it starts.
