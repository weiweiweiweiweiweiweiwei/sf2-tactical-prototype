Background

While the origin of the HAVOC is unknown, it's believed to be a custom build fashioned for a Devotion LMG fan with money to burn. A versatile energy rifle, the HAVOC is meant to mimic the behavior of the Devotion with the increased accuracy of an assault rifle and fewer attachments to reach maximum output. Some might call it “streamlined”, but a certain mystery buyer would probably choose “tailor - made” instead.
